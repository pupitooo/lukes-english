The call: the facts

  • The client: a mid-sized accounting firm, 40 accountants.
  • The problem: since Monday, bank statements from one bank are not imported.
  • Affected: 3 clients of theirs out of 120, about 1,800 transactions.
  • Found in the logs: the bank changed the date format in its export file on Sunday.
  • The fix: the importer accepts both formats; tested on 50 real files, 0 errors.
  • Time: the fix is ready today; re-importing the missing transactions takes about 20 minutes.
  • Risk: a few old files might be imported twice; a duplicate check catches them.
