💻 Class 5: Telling Stories, Present Perfect First

The timeline of a story

step what it does tense example
1. Headline the news, no time yet present perfect I've had the strangest week.
2. When and where the moment you give a time past simple It was last Tuesday, at work.
3. Background what was going on around it past continuous I was fixing a test and everybody was eating.
4. Earlier something before the story past perfect I had pushed a change that morning.
5. Then the events, one after another past simple Then the build broke, so I checked the logs.
6. The turn the surprise past simple That's when I saw it. Somebody had deleted the config.
7. Now the result, back in the present present perfect or present Since then I've always checked the config first.

The one rule

No time word: present perfect. I've changed jobs. I've been to Texas. Something has happened.

A time word: past simple. I changed jobs in October. I was in Texas in 2019. It happened last week.

The headline is present perfect, and the second you say when, the story moves into the past simple and stays there.

A short model

I've broken production exactly once. It was my second week in a new job, a Friday afternoon. Everybody was getting ready to go home and I was finishing a small fix. I had tested it on my laptop, so I pushed it. Ten minutes later, the phones started ringing. It turned out that my fix had switched off the login for every customer. We rolled it back in five minutes, but my boss asked me to explain it on Monday. Since then I've never deployed anything on a Friday.
