One story, seven tenses

steptenseexample from the listening
headline, no timepresent perfectI've found something good.
old news, historypast simple from the startIn 1969 the computer nearly stopped the landing.
backgroundpast continuousThey were coming down toward the surface.
the eventspast simpleA yellow light came on.
earlier than the storypast perfectA few weeks earlier, they'd had a simulation.
a long time before the momentpast perfect continuousThey'd been holding their breath.
back to nowpresent perfectI've never written down a single alarm.

No time word: present perfect (I've lost my keys). A time word, or a story that is clearly over: past simple (I lost my keys yesterday). Czech has one past for both, English has two.

The past perfect is the step back: when you are already in the past and need something even earlier. When I arrived, the meeting had already started.

2lost. yesterday is a time word: past simple.

3Have. ever, no time: Have you ever been.

4went. in 2019 is a time word: past simple.

5've worked. since + a start point: present perfect. Czech uses the present, English does not.

6did. A question with when is a time question: past simple, did.

7was preparing. Background, in the middle of it when something happened: past continuous.

8was reading. while + background: past continuous.

9had already started. Earlier than another past moment: past perfect.

10had deleted. The deleting happened before the couldn't: past perfect.

11had been working. A long action up to a past moment: past perfect continuous.

12had been. It turned out + something true from before: past perfect.
