What they said: reported speech

what he saidwhat you report
I am tired.He said (that) he was tired.
We are debugging it.They said they were debugging it.
I fixed it. / I have fixed it.She told me she had fixed it.
We will change the tool.They said they would change the tool.
We can start earlier.He said they could start earlier.
Where do you work?He asked me where I worked.
Can we finish by Friday?She asked if we could finish by Friday.

said + what: He said that... told + a person: He told me that... Never "he said me", never "he told that".

A reported question is not a question any more: no do, no did, normal word order. He asked me where I worked, nikoliv "where did I work".

If it is still true now, you can keep the tense: He said the service is back. Both are fine.

Part C: your story

Write it (about 150 words) or record it (about 90 seconds) and send it to Lukas. True, from your life or your work. It needs:

  • a headline in the present perfect: I've had the strangest week. / I've nearly broken production.
  • background in the past continuous: We were getting ready for a release.
  • one step back with the past perfect: Somebody had changed the settings.
  • at least three reported sentences: My boss told me that... / The client asked if... / He said he would...
  • an ending back to now: Since then I've...
