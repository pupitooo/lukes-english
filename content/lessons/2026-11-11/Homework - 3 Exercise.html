The modal goes in the result half

EnglishCzech
if you need help, you can call mejestli budeš potřebovat pomoc, můžeš mi zavolat
if you need help, you should call mejestli budeš potřebovat pomoc, měl bys mi zavolat
if I had time, I could help youkdybych měl čas, mohl bych ti pomoct
if I had time, I might help youkdybych měl čas, možná bych ti pomohl
if I had known, I could have helped youkdybych to (tehdy) věděl, mohl jsem ti pomoct
if I had known, I might have helped youkdybych to (tehdy) věděl, možná bych ti pomohl
you should have told meměl jsi mi to říct

The modal sits in the result half, never in the if half: can, could, might, should take the place of will or would. In the past it is modal + have + past participle: could have helped, might have survived.

Flip it and nothing changes but the comma: I could have helped you if you had told me. Find the if half first, wherever it sits, and keep will and would out of it.
