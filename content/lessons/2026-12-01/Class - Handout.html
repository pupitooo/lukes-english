💻 Class 7: The Test, then What They Said

Part 1. Seven classes, one test

  1. When the build ___ (finish), I'll send you the link.
  2. If it ___ (work), we'll ship it on Friday.
  3. I'll call you as soon as I ___ (know) more.
  4. We don't have ___ time before the release.
  5. There were too ___ bugs to ship it.
  6. We need ___ testers than last time. (less / fewer)
  7. Thanks for ___ (help) me with the demo.
  8. I'm interested in ___ (learn) Scala.
  9. If I ___ (have) more time, I'd learn another language.
  10. If we had tested it, we ___ (not lose) the data.
  11. Unless you ___ (restart) it, it won't work.
  12. We could have fixed it if somebody ___ (tell) us.
  13. I ___ (work) here since October.
  14. I ___ (lose) my laptop yesterday.
  15. When I arrived, the meeting ___ already ___ (start).
  16. While I ___ (read) the logs, the server crashed.
  17. It turned out that somebody ___ (delete) the config.
  18. I ___ never ___ (be) to Texas.
  19. I'm used to ___ (work) with bad English.
  20. It feels as ___ it were much faster.

Part 2. What they said

what he said what you report
I am tired. He said (that) he was tired.
We are debugging it. They said they were debugging it.
I fixed it. / I have fixed it. She told me she had fixed it.
We will change the tool. They said they would change the tool.
We can start earlier. He said they could start earlier.
Where do you work? He asked me where I worked.
Can we finish by Friday? She asked if we could finish by Friday.

said + what: He said that... told + a person: He told me that...

A reported question has normal word order: He asked me where I worked.

the verb the pattern example
explain explain that She explained that the export was slow.
promise promise to + verb He promised to send the logs.
warn warn somebody that They warned us that the date was tight.
suggest suggest + -ing She suggested moving the demo.
ask ask somebody to + verb He asked me to check the numbers.
agree agree to + verb They agreed to pay for the extra week.

Part 3. The client call, word for word

  1. "The export is too slow."
  2. "We need it by the end of January."
  3. "We tested the beta last week."
  4. "We have found two bugs in the reports."
  5. "We will pay for one extra week, not two."
  6. "Can you show it to our boss on Thursday?"
  7. "Why does it need a new server?"
  8. "Please send us the release notes."
  9. "Don't change the login screen."
  10. "Our accountants are very happy with the new screen."
